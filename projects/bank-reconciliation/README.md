# Bank Reconciliation

**Portfolio project · Practice case · Fictional data**

## Business question

Can book and bank transactions be reconciled systematically, with timing differences and bank-only items clearly identified and followed through to cashbook treatment?

## Dataset

Source: `datasets/bank_reconciliation.csv`

The dataset contains ten August 2026 transactions with matched items, deposits in transit, outstanding cheques, bank charges and bank interest.

> **Public-safe note:** all figures are fictional and are provided for portfolio practice.

## Reconciliation workflow

1. Compare book and bank amounts.
2. Match transactions using reference and amount.
3. Identify unmatched items.
4. Classify timing differences and bank-only transactions.
5. Determine which items require cashbook adjustment.
6. Calculate adjusted book balance.
7. Calculate adjusted bank balance.
8. Confirm the adjusted balances agree.
9. Document proposed actions and control implications.

## Required portfolio evidence

- Completed Excel reconciliation
- Reconciliation statement
- Exception log
- Proposed cashbook adjustments
- Control commentary

## Exception categories to test

| Category | Treatment to investigate |
|---|---|
| Deposit in transit | Timing difference |
| Outstanding cheque | Timing difference |
| Bank charge | Potential cashbook adjustment |
| Bank interest | Potential cashbook adjustment |
| Matched item | No reconciling adjustment |

Do not assume treatment without checking the underlying reconciliation logic.

## Control commentary

Address:

- Frequency of reconciliation
- Independent review
- Ageing of outstanding items
- Evidence retention
- Escalation of unusual or stale items
- Separation of preparation and review
