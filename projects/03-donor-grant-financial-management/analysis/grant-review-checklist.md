# Grant Financial Review Checklist

| Review area | Key checks | Evidence / output |
|---|---|---|
| Grant setup | Agreement, dates, budget, amendments | Approved grant file |
| Budget | Current approved budget and coding | Budget reconciliation |
| GL | Completeness, coding and reconciliations | GL review |
| Expenditure | Allowability, allocability, reasonableness | Exception log |
| Reporting | Partner/donor reports agree to records | Reporting reconciliation |
| Advances | Outstanding balances and ageing | Advance schedule |
| Commitments | Open commitments and purchase orders | Commitment review |
| Cash | Current balance, receipts and payments | Cash forecast |
| Forecast | Remaining activity and expected costs | Updated forecast |
| Compliance | Supporting documents and eligibility | Compliance exceptions |
| Partner | Partner burn rate and reporting quality | Partner action tracker |
| Close-out | Final invoices, accruals and unspent balances | Close-out checklist |

## Exception rating approach

Use factual categories rather than subjective labels:

- **Correction required** — accounting or documentation issue that can be resolved.
- **Follow-up required** — explanation or evidence is incomplete.
- **Potential compliance exception** — expenditure may require donor review under the applicable agreement.
- **Management escalation** — issue may materially affect delivery, funding, liquidity or compliance.

## Review questions

1. Does expenditure agree to the accounting records?
2. Are costs within the applicable grant period?
3. Are supporting documents available?
4. Are partner reports reconciled to finance records?
5. Are cash requests supported by realistic near-term needs?
6. Are commitments included in the forecast?
7. Are material variances explained?
8. Are corrective actions assigned and tracked?

This checklist is a portfolio demonstration and should be adapted to the specific donor agreement and organisational policy.
