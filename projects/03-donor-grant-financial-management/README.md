# Donor Grant Financial Management

## Purpose

This case study demonstrates a structured finance review for donor-funded programmes, covering grant compliance, financial reporting, partner oversight, cash management and forecasting.

The framework is intentionally generic and does not reproduce any confidential donor requirements.

## Grant finance review framework

### 1. Grant setup and budget

Confirm:

- approved grant budget;
- grant start and end dates;
- approved cost categories;
- budget amendments;
- donor restrictions;
- reporting calendar;
- applicable indirect-cost or cost-share requirements.

### 2. Financial reporting

Review:

- general ledger activity;
- trial balance and reconciliations;
- budget versus actual expenditure;
- supporting documentation;
- accruals and prepayments;
- outstanding advances;
- intercompany or cross-charged costs where applicable.

### 3. Donor compliance

Test whether expenditure is:

- allowable under the applicable agreement;
- allocable to the grant;
- reasonable and supported;
- incurred within the eligible period;
- correctly coded and documented.

### 4. Partner financial oversight

Review:

- partner financial reports;
- budget utilisation;
- supporting documentation;
- cash requests;
- outstanding advances;
- open commitments;
- exception items;
- corrective-action status.

### 5. Cash and forecast

Assess:

- current cash position;
- upcoming receipts;
- planned payments;
- funding gaps;
- burn rate;
- remaining-period forecast;
- timing of major commitments.

## Exceptions and escalation

A strong finance review should clearly distinguish between:

1. items requiring routine correction;
2. items requiring additional documentation;
3. potential donor-compliance exceptions;
4. forecast or liquidity risks; and
5. issues requiring management escalation.

## Finance Manager output

The final review should give management a concise view of:

**What happened → why it matters → what action is required → who owns it → when it should be resolved.**
