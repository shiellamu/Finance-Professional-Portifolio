# Budget vs Actual Management Report

## Executive financial position

| Measure | Position |
|---|---:|
| Annual budget | 1,100,000 |
| Actual expenditure | 896,000 |
| Underspend | 204,000 |
| Utilisation | 81.5% |

### Management commentary

Expenditure is below the annual budget by 204,000. The position requires interpretation alongside programme delivery because an underspend can reflect delayed implementation, procurement timing, revised activities, or genuine cost reductions.

## Main variance messages

**Travel and field operations — 54,000 underspend**

This is the largest absolute variance. Finance should confirm whether field activities were delayed, rescheduled, reduced or delivered through a different approach.

**Training and workshops — 49,000 underspend**

Utilisation is 65.0%. Management should review the training calendar, procurement status and expected delivery dates before reducing the forecast.

**Monitoring and evaluation — 48,000 underspend**

The variance should be assessed against planned evaluation milestones and the implementation calendar.

**Subcontracts — 15,000 underspend**

Utilisation is 92.9%. Outstanding commitments, contract values and expected invoices should be monitored because remaining budget is relatively limited.

## Forecast implications

The next forecast should incorporate:

- confirmed implementation dates;
- open commitments;
- purchase orders and procurement status;
- expected invoices;
- approved programme changes;
- risks that could accelerate or defer expenditure.

## Recommended management actions

| Priority | Action | Owner | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm reasons for material travel, training and M&E variances | Programme + Finance | Next review |
| 2 | Reconcile commitments and expected invoices | Finance | Before forecast update |
| 3 | Update remaining-period forecast | Finance + Programme | Next forecast cycle |
| 4 | Confirm whether delayed activities remain within grant period | Programme | Next programme review |
| 5 | Escalate material delivery or funding risks | Finance Manager / Programme Lead | As required |

## Decision-support conclusion

The key management question is not simply whether spending is below budget. It is whether the remaining budget is still aligned with the programme activities that management expects to deliver.
