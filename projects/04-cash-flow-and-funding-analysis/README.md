# Cash Flow & Funding Analysis

## Purpose

This case study demonstrates how finance teams can use a rolling cash forecast to identify liquidity pressure, anticipate funding gaps and manage payment timing.

The model uses synthetic monthly data.

## Core questions

- What is the projected closing cash balance?
- When is liquidity lowest?
- Which receipts and payments drive the movement?
- Can payment timing be managed without disrupting implementation?
- When should funding or management action be initiated?

## Key result

The lowest projected closing cash balance in the six-month model is **45,000 in May**.

This does not by itself establish a funding shortfall. Finance should compare the forecast with minimum operating cash requirements, committed payments, expected receipts and funding arrangements.

## Management actions

1. Validate expected receipts and their timing.
2. Confirm whether major payments can be scheduled against available funding.
3. Review open commitments and distinguish committed from discretionary spend.
4. Escalate a potential liquidity gap early enough to allow funding action.
5. Refresh the forecast when material assumptions change.
