# Financial Statement Analysis

**Portfolio project · Practice case · Fictional data**

## Business question

How are profitability, liquidity and shareholder returns changing over time, and which movements warrant management investigation?

## Dataset

Source: `datasets/financial_statements.csv`

The dataset contains six quarterly periods from 2025-Q1 to 2026-Q2 and includes revenue, cost of sales, operating expenses, interest, tax, selected current assets/liabilities, total assets and equity.

> **Public-safe note:** all figures are fictional and are provided for portfolio practice.

## Analysis scope

Build the analysis in Excel and document your reasoning:

- Revenue and growth trend
- Gross profit and gross margin
- Operating profit and operating margin
- Net profit
- Current ratio
- ROE
- Quarter-on-quarter movement
- Management observations and follow-up questions

### Core formulas

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Gross profit | Revenue − Cost of Sales |
| Operating profit | Gross Profit − Operating Expenses |
| Net profit | Operating Profit − Interest Expense − Tax Expense |
| Gross margin | Gross Profit ÷ Revenue |
| Operating margin | Operating Profit ÷ Revenue |
| Current ratio | (Cash + Receivables + Inventory) ÷ Current Liabilities |
| ROE | Net Profit ÷ Total Equity |

## Suggested workbook structure

`Raw_Data` → `Calculations` → `KPI_Summary` → `Charts` → `Management_Summary`

Keep source data unchanged and separate from calculations.

## Management commentary framework

For each material movement:

**What happened → How material is it? → What might explain it? → What should be investigated next?**

Do not present unsupported explanations as facts.

## Limitations

This is a synthetic case. The dataset does not provide notes to the accounts, industry benchmarks, cash-flow statements, accounting policies or operational drivers. Conclusions should therefore remain limited to the available data.
