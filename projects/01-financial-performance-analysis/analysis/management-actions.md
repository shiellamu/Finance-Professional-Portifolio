# Management Actions

| Issue | Finance review | Management action |
|---|---|---|
| Travel underspend | Confirm field activity schedule and commitments | Reforecast based on confirmed implementation |
| Training underspend | Review training calendar and procurement status | Identify delayed activities and revised delivery dates |
| M&E underspend | Compare spend with evaluation milestones | Confirm whether activities remain planned |
| High subcontract utilisation | Review contracts, commitments and expected invoices | Monitor remaining funding and payment timing |
| Overall underspend | Separate timing variances from genuine savings | Update forecast before releasing or reallocating funds |

## Questions for programme teams

1. Which activities have been delayed?
2. Which activities are still expected to occur within the grant period?
3. Are there approved changes to the implementation plan?
4. What commitments have already been made but not yet invoiced?
5. Are procurement delays affecting planned expenditure?
6. Which variances are expected to reverse later in the period?
7. Which variances represent genuine changes in programme cost?

## Finance Manager perspective

A finance manager should avoid presenting an underspend as a positive outcome without understanding the programme context. The analysis should help management decide whether to maintain or revise the forecast, reschedule activities, investigate implementation bottlenecks, or escalate a potential funding or delivery risk.

The objective is not merely to explain the numbers. It is to connect financial performance with programme delivery and management decisions.
