## CV quality fixes

1. Replace the incomplete phrase **“with years of experience”** with the verified experience period or a neutral phrase such as **“Finance professional with experience…”**.
2. Keep the title **Finance Professional** as the broad positioning and use **HQ Accountant** / **Finance Analyst** as the actual employment titles.
3. Consolidate repetitive bullets so the strongest evidence is easier to scan.
4. Preserve the specific, verifiable **18 implementing partners** achievement from the Grants Officer role.
5. Add quantified outcomes to process-improvement bullets only when the figures can be verified.
6. Keep confidential employer, donor, partner and programme information out of the public portfolio.
7. Keep the CV, LinkedIn profile and GitHub career page consistent on titles, dates, qualifications and certification.
