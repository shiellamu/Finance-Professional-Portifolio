# Portfolio Publishing Standard

## Before anything is published

- Confirm the material is owned by Shiella or licensed for publication.
- Remove passwords, tokens, API keys and private credentials.
- Remove confidential employer, donor, partner and client information.
- Remove unnecessary personal identifiers from certificates and documents.
- Label fictional and anonymised datasets clearly.
- Check formulas, figures, dates and professional claims.
- Confirm links work.
- Keep the CV, LinkedIn profile and website consistent.

## Evidence hierarchy

Highest confidence: authentic credential evidence and directly reproducible portfolio work.

Public-safe demonstrations: fictional or anonymised datasets with transparent methodology.

Descriptions: claims should remain factual and supportable.

## Intellectual property

The portfolio is public so employers and professional contacts can review the work. Public visibility does not grant unrestricted permission to reproduce the original work.
